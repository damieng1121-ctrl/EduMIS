## 1. Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement:

- **"UK GDPR"** means the UK General Data Protection Regulation as it forms part of the
  law of England and Wales, Scotland, and Northern Ireland.
- **"Data Protection Legislation"** means the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and
  any successor or related UK legislation, together with all applicable guidance issued
  by the Information Commissioner's Office ("**ICO**").
- **"Personal Data"**, **"Processing"**, **"Controller"**, **"Processor"**, **"Data
  Subject"**, and **"Personal Data Breach"** have the meanings given in the UK GDPR.
- **"Services"** means the EduMIS system as provided by the Processor to the School
  under the Principal Agreement.
- **"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by the Processor to Process
  Personal Data in connection with providing the Services.

## 2. Roles of the parties

**2.1** The School is the **Controller** of the Personal Data described in Schedule 1;
the Processor Processes that Personal Data solely on the Controller's behalf as
**Processor**, for the purpose of providing the Services.

**2.2** Nothing in this Agreement relieves the School of its own obligations as
Controller under the Data Protection Legislation, including its obligations to Data
Subjects (pupils, parents/guardians, and staff) directly.

## 3. Subject matter and duration of processing

**3.1** The subject matter of the Processing is the operation of the EduMIS school
management information system on the School's behalf, as more particularly described in
Schedule 1.

**3.2** Processing under this Agreement continues for the duration of the Principal
Agreement, and thereafter only to the extent necessary to comply with Clause 10
(return/deletion of data) or a legal requirement to retain data for a further period.

## 4. Nature and purpose of processing

The Processor Processes Personal Data on the School's behalf for the purpose of
providing a school management information system covering: pupil record-keeping;
attendance recording; behaviour and welfare management (including SEND); assessment
tracking; school operations (meal registers, clubs, staff records, pastoral
interventions); parents' evening scheduling; parent-facing communication (email, and SMS
where configured); statutory-return-adjacent reporting (school census pre-flight checks);
Common Transfer File (CTF) import/export when a pupil joins or leaves the School; and,
where the School elects to enable it, roster synchronisation via the Wonde integration.
This is set out in full in Schedule 1.

## 5. Types of personal data and categories of data subjects

Set out in full in **Schedule 1**. In summary, the Processor Processes:

- **Pupils**: identity and demographic data (name, DOB, gender, ethnicity, home
  language, nationality, address), UPN and school-history data, SEND status and SEND
  plans (**special category data**), safeguarding-related behaviour and accident records
  (**special category data**), assessment results, attendance records, and free-text
  medical notes (**special category data**).
- **Parents/guardians**: name, contact details, relationship to pupil, parental
  responsibility and collection permissions, login credentials, and message content sent
  to them.
- **Staff**: name, contact details, role, job title, and — where employed by the School
  — Single Central Record / vetting data (DBS check details, right-to-work evidence,
  barred-list check, prohibition-from-teaching check, references, overseas checks) —
  **special category and criminal-offence-related data**.

## 6. Processor obligations

The Processor shall:

**6.1** Process Personal Data only on the School's documented instructions (including
as set out in this Agreement and the Principal Agreement), unless required to do
otherwise by UK law — in which case the Processor shall (unless prohibited by law) inform
the School of that legal requirement before Processing.

**6.2** Ensure that all persons authorised to Process the Personal Data (including
employees, contractors, and agents) are subject to a binding duty of confidentiality.

**6.3** Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of
security appropriate to the risk, in accordance with Article 32 UK GDPR, including as
applicable: pseudonymisation and encryption of Personal Data (see Schedule 2 for the
specific measures currently implemented); the ability to ensure ongoing confidentiality,
integrity, availability, and resilience of processing systems; the ability to restore
availability and access to Personal Data in a timely manner following an incident; and a
process for regularly testing and evaluating the effectiveness of these measures.

**6.4** Not engage another processor (a **Sub-processor**) without the School's prior
specific or general written authorisation. Where general authorisation is given, the
Processor shall inform the School of any intended changes concerning the addition or
replacement of Sub-processors, giving the School the opportunity to object. The current
list of Sub-processors is set out in **Schedule 3**.

**6.5** Ensure that any Sub-processor is bound by data protection obligations
equivalent to those set out in this Agreement, and remain fully liable to the School for
the performance of that Sub-processor's obligations.

**6.6** Taking into account the nature of the Processing, assist the School by
appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, for the
fulfilment of the School's obligation to respond to requests for exercising a Data
Subject's rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection)
laid down in Chapter III of the UK GDPR.

**6.7** Assist the School in ensuring compliance with its obligations relating to the
security of Processing, breach notification, and Data Protection Impact Assessments
(including prior consultation with the ICO where required), taking into account the
nature of Processing and the information available to the Processor.

**6.8** Notify the School **without undue delay, and in any event within [X HOURS] of
becoming aware**, of any Personal Data Breach affecting the Personal Data Processed
under this Agreement, providing at minimum: the nature of the breach; the categories and
approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned; the likely consequences; and
the measures taken or proposed to address it. *[The exact notification window is a
negotiated term — the School's own duty to notify the ICO within 72 hours of becoming
aware of a breach means the Processor's window to the School must be materially shorter
than that; a common starting point is 24–48 hours, but this must be agreed and inserted
here, not left as a placeholder in the executed contract.]*

**6.9** At the School's election, at the end of the provision of the Services, delete
or return all Personal Data to the School, and delete existing copies unless UK law
requires the Processor to retain the Personal Data — see Clause 10.

**6.10** Make available to the School all information reasonably necessary to
demonstrate compliance with this Agreement and the obligations laid down in Article 28
UK GDPR, and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the
School or an auditor mandated by the School, on reasonable notice.

**6.11** Immediately inform the School if, in the Processor's opinion, an instruction
from the School infringes the Data Protection Legislation.

## 7. School (Controller) obligations

The School shall:

**7.1** Ensure it has a valid lawful basis (and, where relying on consent, a valid
consent mechanism) for all Personal Data it instructs the Processor to Process.

**7.2** Provide the Processor with clear, documented instructions for Processing, and
ensure those instructions are themselves lawful.

**7.3** Comply with its own transparency obligations to pupils, parents/guardians, and
staff (e.g. a privacy notice) covering the Processing carried out via the Services.

## Schedule 2: Technical and organisational measures

*[To be reviewed and expanded by the Processor's technical lead and the School's DPO.
Reflects measures actually implemented in the system at the time of drafting — verify
against the live deployment before relying on this list.]*

- Role-based access control, scoped per school (`Tenant`), enforced server-side on every
  request from the authenticated session — not from client-supplied identifiers.
- Mandatory two-factor authentication (TOTP) for elevated staff roles (Tenant Admin,
  Trust Admin, Super Admin); optional for other staff.
- Rate-limiting on login and 2FA verification attempts to reduce brute-force risk.
- TOTP secrets and third-party API tokens (e.g. the Wonde integration) encrypted at rest
  using AES-256-GCM.
- An audit log recording who viewed or changed pupil, SEND, and staff-vetting records,
  and when.
- Confidentiality gating on safeguarding- and bullying-category behaviour records,
  restricted to senior/safeguarding-designated staff.
- No self-service account creation for staff or parents — every account is provisioned
  by an administrator or via domain-matched single sign-on.
- **Known gaps at the time of drafting** (to be closed before, or tracked openly
  alongside, a production deployment): no field-level encryption of free-text special
  category fields (e.g. medical notes) at rest — reliant on database access control and
  the hosting provider's encryption-at-rest; no automated backup process; no automated
  data retention/deletion process. See the System README for current status.
